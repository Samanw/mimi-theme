Soft wool, warm beige and quiet curves — Palmina is a calm, generous seat built for slow evenings. The deep cushion rests on a solid ash frame, hand-finished with natural oil and built to be repaired, not replaced.

Palmina began as a study in how little a chair needs to give everything. Its frame is solid ash, joined without visible hardware and finished in natural oil that deepens with the years. The seat and back are upholstered in a dense wool bouclé that keeps its texture long after the first afternoon nap.

Every cover is sewn to order and can be removed for cleaning, and every cushion is filled with a feather-wrapped foam core that holds its shape without going hard. The proportions are generous without being wide — it slots into reading corners, bedrooms and the quiet end of the living room with equal ease.

Highlights

  • Solid ash frame, hand-oiled and fully repairable
  • Dense wool bouclé cover, removable and washable
  • Feather-wrapped foam cushions that keep their shape
  • Made to order in small batches — delivery in 2–3 weeks
